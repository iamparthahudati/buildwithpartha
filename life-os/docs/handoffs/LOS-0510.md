## Completed behavior

Added `LoginScreen`, composing the existing form primitives named in the ticket into the actual login screen on top of LOS-0508's `useLogin` / `resolveReturnTarget` and LOS-0505's `POST /auth/login`.

- Composes `FormFieldGroup`/`FormErrorSummary`/`FormField` (LOS-0401), `TextInput`/`PasswordInput` (LOS-0314/0315), `Button` (LOS-0306), `Alert` (LOS-0408), and `Link` (LOS-0308) directly — no new form primitive was written.
- **Keyboard and password-manager support**: `PasswordInput` sets `autoComplete="current-password"`; `TextInput` for email sets `type="email"` and `autoComplete="email"`. The form has `noValidate` and standard keyboard submission.
- **Links and navigation**: Includes a direct link to the forgot-password route (`/life-os/forgot-password`) and signup route (`/life-os/signup`), constructed dynamically from `readPublicEnvironment().appBasePath`.
- **Safe return-to redirection**: On login success, reads `returnTo` from query parameters and calls `resolveReturnTarget(returnTo)`, navigating safely to the intended destination (e.g. `/life-os/app/tasks`) or falling back to `/life-os/app/today` for missing/malformed/open-redirect attempts. `navigate` prop is overridable for clean testing.
- **Enumeration safety and generic error handling**: An invalid email/password combination reports `401 INVALID_CREDENTIALS` from the backend and renders the approved generic alert message `"Email or password is incorrect."` without blaming the user or distinguishing unknown email vs wrong password.
- **Rate limiting and recoverable failure**: `429 RATE_LIMITED` renders `"Too many attempts. Wait a few minutes before trying again."` and unexpected errors render `"We couldn't sign you in. Your details are still here."` as an `Alert` (with `announce="alert"` and auto-focus) while preserving entered values.
- **Inline + summary errors**: Client-side validation (`validateLoginForm`) and backend `VALIDATION_FAILED` field errors are mapped via `resolveLoginFieldError` and focused through `FormErrorSummary` using the callback ref focus pattern to handle `FormField`'s async registration race cleanly.
- **Pending submission deduplication**: Duplicate submits while a request is in flight are ignored, and the primary submit button displays `loading={loginMutation.isPending}`.
