## Completed behavior

- Built `TaskDetailsHeader` as a reusable presentation component for the future Task Details route and aggregation response.
- Renders deep-link breadcrumbs, Task title and description, canonical status and priority, MIT/archive/deleted/overdue/blocker context, Project, timezone-aware due date, Estimate, Time spent, progress, and Labels.
