## Completed behavior

Built the independent Today reflection and capture widgets required before LOS-0614 composes the complete mocked Today screen.

- `TodayReviewPrompt` renders morning and evening Daily Review rows from the canonical `NOT_STARTED`, `DRAFT`, `FINALIZED`, and `SKIPPED` states. Each row exposes the matching Start, Resume, or Open Review route, labels the expected duration, and may identify the caller-selected locally appropriate period as `Suggested now`.
- Review loading reserves the final two-row layout. A refresh may carry last-known data, keeping finalized/skipped facts and saved Draft actions visible. An isolated error offers Retry and explicitly says when the last saved state remains shown; it never replaces another Today widget or claims a Draft was lost.
- `TodayBrainCapture` is a controlled compact form with the approved `What is on your mind?` prompt, whitespace validation, an independent unprocessed Brain Dump Item count, and a canonical Brain Dump link.
- Capture requests explicitly distinguish online `create` from offline `device-draft`. Saving keeps the action stable; success is quiet and factual; errors keep the caller-owned text in place; offline drafts state that only device storage is confirmed. A count failure does not disable capture and has its own Retry action.
- The capture component intentionally does not put private text in localStorage or implement storage internally. Its integration caller owns the privacy contract for a user/session-scoped IndexedDB draft/queue, expiry, account-switch/sign-out cleanup, and eventual sync.
- Development catalog specimens cover morning prompt, evening Draft, preserved Review error, interactive capture success, failed capture with preserved text, count failure, and offline device draft.
- Both components use real headings, lists, labels, forms, links, buttons, live status/error semantics, token-only styles, responsive stacking, and caller-owned canonical routes/data.
