### Backend — New timezone and recurrence matrix test suite (`TimezoneRecurrenceMatrixIntegrationTests.java`)

Created a pure domain-layer JUnit 5 test class (no Spring context, no database) covering all six sections of the timezone and recurrence acceptance matrix:

- **Section 1 — Timezone class correctness**:
  - `America/New_York` spring-forward 2026-03-08: daily series includes the gap date exactly once (does not skip it)
  - `America/New_York` fall-back 2026-11-01: daily series includes Nov 1 exactly once (no duplicate from the 25-hour day)
  - `Asia/Kolkata` (+05:30 half-hour, no DST): daily series produces correct sequence across UK spring-forward dates
  - `UTC` baseline: consecutive calendar days reference
  - `Europe/London` spring-forward 2026-03-29: daily series includes the gap date exactly once

- **Section 2 — Midnight and year boundary correctness**:
  - Daily series crosses Dec 31 → Jan 1 year boundary without skipping or duplicating
  - Weekly MONDAY series from Dec 28, 2026 to Jan 11, 2027 spans the ISO week/year boundary
  - Weekly MONDAY+FRIDAY: first Friday of a series starting Dec 28, 2026 falls in 2027 (Jan 1) within the same week iteration

- **Section 3 — Month-end and leap-year clamping**:
  - Monthly day-31 from Jan 31, 2024: Feb 2024 (leap year) clamps to Feb 29; Apr 2024 clamps to Apr 30
  - Monthly day-29 from Jan 29, 2025: Feb 2025 (non-leap) clamps to Feb 28
  - Monthly day-31 UNTIL_DATE ending exactly on Mar 31: includes Mar 31, excludes Apr
  - Monthly day-29 starting Feb 29, 2024 (leap): next Feb in 2025 clamps to Feb 28

- **Section 4 — Week/year ISO boundary recurrence**:
  - Weekly MONDAY from Dec 29, 2025: produces Dec 29, Jan 5, Jan 12 (spanning the ISO week/year join)
  - Bi-weekly WEDNESDAY from Dec 24, 2025: skips Dec 31 week, lands on Jan 7 2026
  - YEARLY frequency gap documented as `@Disabled` test with TODO referencing the known limitation

- **Section 5 — Timezone change on recurring series**:
  - Timezone annotation change (`America/New_York` → `UTC`) does not alter the `LocalDate` occurrence sequence
  - Half-hour offset (`Asia/Kolkata`) vs integer-offset (`Asia/Tashkent`) produces identical `LocalDate` sequence

- **Section 6 — Habit streak correctness across DST boundary**:
  - Daily habit in `America/New_York`: unbroken 8-day streak across spring-forward 2026-03-08 (no gap)
  - Daily habit in `America/New_York`: fall-back 2026-11-01 not counted twice (5 eligible periods, all met)
  - Daily habit in `Asia/Kolkata`: streak stable across month boundary (5 eligible, all met)
  - Pause spanning DST spring-forward day (Mar 8) excludes that day without breaking the surrounding streak

## Known limitations or follow-ups

- **YEARLY frequency**: `RecurrenceOccurrenceEngine` does not implement `YEARLY` recurrence. The gap is documented with `@Disabled` test `TZ-B-4.3` and listed as a known limitation in `docs/49-TIMEZONE-RECURRENCE-MATRIX.md`. A future ticket should implement YEARLY with leap-year Feb 29 → Feb 28 clamping and add it to the recurrence editor UI.
- **Half-hour display rounding** in `Asia/Kathmandu` (UTC+05:45): cosmetic off-by-one minute in some browser locale formatters. No data integrity impact; accepted as cosmetic.
- **Timezone-change retroactivity**: stored `LocalDate` habit entries are never re-bucketed when a user changes their timezone. This is by design and matches the contract established in `HabitStreakCalculationsTests`.
- iOS Safari `position: fixed` virtual keyboard shift near midnight DST transitions: inherited from LOS-1504; monitored.
