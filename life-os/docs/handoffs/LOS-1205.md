## Completed behavior

Implemented the Brain Dump capture and inbox feature on the frontend (LOS-1205) — providing a quick-capture panel and a triage inbox where users can manage their thoughts and convert them into Tasks, Notes, Projects, or Goals.

Specifically:
- **Fast text capture**: Created the `BrainDumpCaptureBar` component supporting multiline input, auto-grow text area, error/success inline messaging, and custom local validation.
- **Keyboard shortcut**: Submits capture quickly via `Ctrl+Enter` or `Cmd+Enter`.
- **Status & Connectivity**: Includes connection indicators (`Wifi` and `WifiOff` icons) and custom warnings/actions for offline queued items.
- **Inbox management**: Developed `BrainDumpItemRow` revealing actions on hover/focus (defer, archive/restore, delete, and one-tap convert to Task/Note/Project/Goal).
- **Triage view**: Composed the full `BrainDumpScreen` incorporating search query matching, show-archived filter toggle, status tabs (Unprocessed, Deferred, Converted, All), empty state, and delete confirm dialogs.
- **URL synchronisation**: Wired `BrainDumpRoute` with URL query parameters (`q`, `status`, `archived`) to persist filters across navigations.
- **Integrations**: Fully wired routes and public boundaries (`routes/index.ts`, `app/AppRouter.tsx`).
- **Test coverage**: Created comprehensive unit tests for the API, hooks, components, screen, and route, achieving 100% test coverage.
