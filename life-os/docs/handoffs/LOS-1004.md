## Completed behavior

- Added authenticated, CSRF-protected `GET`/`POST /weekly-plans`, `GET`/`PUT /weekly-plans/{id}`, and `POST /weekly-plans/{id}/finalize` and `/reopen` endpoints. Reads are owner-scoped; missing and cross-user Weekly Plans and Tasks remain indistinguishable.
- A required anchor `weekDate` is normalized with the Account's stored IANA timezone and ISO week-start preference. The resulting timezone, week-start day and inclusive seven-local-date bounds are frozen on the revision so later preference changes cannot reinterpret history.
- Drafts store seven explicit daily capacity decisions, ordered user-authored outcomes, and up to 500 unique owned Task allocations. Each item may link to an outcome, carry planned minutes, and either select a date inside the week or remain explicitly unscheduled. Omitted capacity dates remain zero instead of receiving invented availability.
- Draft responses calculate a live conflict/preflight summary: total planned/capacity minutes, overcapacity minutes/dates, pairwise overlaps among non-cancelled Time Blocks in exact timezone-derived instants, unscheduled items and outcomes without items. Warnings remain visible but do not auto-remove work or prevent an intentional finalization.
- Finalization is versioned and retry-safe. It refreshes current Task title/status values, freezes those Task snapshots plus the live warning summary, and makes the revision immutable. Reopen requires the current Finalized version and creates one editable successor revision with remapped outcome/item identities and an explicit predecessor link.
- Account-row locking serializes create/finalize/reopen decisions. The service and V20 constraints enforce one Draft per Account/week, same-owner Task links, outcome membership, valid week-local dates/minute bounds, monotonic revisions and complete Finalized snapshots.
