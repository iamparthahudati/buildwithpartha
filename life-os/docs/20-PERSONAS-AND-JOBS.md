# Personas and top jobs

Status: planning baseline validated from the owner's “my life project” intent and supplied productivity references. Revalidate after the owner uses the first working release; assumptions are marked.

## Primary persona — Partha, independent builder

### Context

Balances software/product work, learning, personal administration, health routines, longer-term financial/career goals and several active projects. Work varies between planned deep-focus sessions and quick incoming obligations.

### Needs

- See what matters today without rebuilding a plan from several tools.
- Keep project outcomes, concrete tasks and reserved time connected.
- Protect focus while still capturing interruptions quickly.
- Decide what rolls forward rather than carrying an unexplained overdue pile.
- Review whether time and action match goals.
- Keep highly personal data private and portable.

### Barriers

- Planning overhead can exceed the value of planning.
- Dense dashboards can become noise when everything looks urgent.
- A perfect schedule breaks when new work arrives.
- Habit/streak tools can create guilt instead of insight.
- Duplicate records across tasks/calendar/notes become inconsistent.

### Success

Within roughly two minutes, the user can understand the day, choose/change one MIT, schedule or start the next action, and capture anything distracting them. At week end, unfinished work is deliberately completed, rescheduled, carried or dropped.

## Secondary persona — Structured professional

Runs several concurrent work/personal projects with deadlines. Needs dependable filters, milestones, recurring responsibilities, calendar planning, reports, notifications and export. Values consistency, keyboard efficiency and predictable recovery from mistakes.

## Secondary persona — Gradual adopter

Starts with Brain Dump, notes, a few tasks and habits. Needs a useful sparse Today screen, progressive onboarding and no requirement to populate every module. Adopts projects, scheduling and reviews only when they solve a present problem.

## Accessibility situations

Any persona may use keyboard-only interaction, screen reader, touch, magnification/200% zoom, reduced motion or higher contrast. They may also be tired, interrupted or using a small phone. These are normal operating conditions, not exceptional personas.

## Ranked jobs to be done

| Rank | When… | I want to… | So I can… | Success signal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | I begin or re-plan a day | see commitments and choose one MIT | start the right work rather than reorganizing lists | MIT and next block/action selected quickly |
| 2 | I decide to do meaningful work | turn a task into protected time and start focus | make progress without losing context | task, block and focus session remain linked |
| 3 | Work arrives while I am focused | capture it in seconds | return attention without forgetting it | Brain Dump/Quick Add saved or honestly queued |
| 4 | A project feels unclear | see outcome, milestones, tasks, health and next action | decide what moves it forward | project has a visible next task and realistic state |
| 5 | My plan changes | move/reschedule work and resolve conflicts | keep the plan realistic | no silent overlap or lost task |
| 6 | I finish or pause work | record completion/time/result | keep progress trustworthy | all linked views reconcile after refresh |
| 7 | I close a day/week/month | review outcomes and unfinished work | learn and make deliberate carry-over decisions | review snapshot and next plan saved |
| 8 | I pursue a longer goal | connect goals to projects/tasks/habits/check-ins | see whether activity serves the outcome | calculation is understandable and current |
| 9 | I need past context | search private projects/tasks/notes/activity | recover information without browsing every module | relevant account-scoped result opens correctly |
| 10 | I change device or leave the product | sign in, sync, export or delete safely | retain control over my personal data | no cross-account/cache leak; portable export works |

## Anti-jobs

LifeOS should not encourage the user to maintain every module, fill every hour, chase meaningless streaks, optimize unmeasured metrics, or accept AI-made changes without review.

## Assumptions to validate through use

- A single owner account is enough for v1.
- Today, Tasks, Projects, Time Blocks and Focus deliver the highest early value.
- Daily review should remain short; weekly review may be the deeper planning ritual.
- Reports become useful only after sufficient history and must not dominate early navigation.
- Mobile is primarily capture/check/complete; complex planning is easier on larger screens, but no core action may be desktop-only.

## Validation plan

After the first runnable shell and again after the core execution release, conduct an owner walkthrough using the ranked jobs. Record time-to-complete, confusion, unused fields, missing recovery, and whether terminology matches real routines. Create change tickets rather than silently rewriting this baseline.
